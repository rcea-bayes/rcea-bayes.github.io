The workshop

The RCEA Bayesian Econometrics Workshop brings together researchers who develop and apply Bayesian methods in economics and finance. It is a small, single-track meeting with time for discussion after every talk.

The 17th edition is hosted by the Department of Economics at the Paris Lodron University of Salzburg, on behalf of the Rimini Centre for Economic Analysis (RCEA). Salzburg lies at the edge of the Alps, and its historic centre is a UNESCO World Heritage site.

Call for papers

Papers in all areas of Bayesian econometrics are welcome for the contributed sessions. This includes macroeconometrics, microeconometrics and financial econometrics, as well as work on Bayesian computation and statistical methodology with economic applications.

How to submit

Please send either a full paper or an extended abstract of up to 500 words as a single PDF to WORKSHOP EMAIL ADDRESS, with “Submission” in the subject line. The PDF should state the paper title, the names and affiliations of all authors, the presenting author and their contact details, keywords and JEL codes.

Getting to Salzburg

  • By plane. Salzburg Airport (SZG) is about 20 minutes from the city centre by bus or taxi. Munich Airport (MUC) is a good alternative, about two hours away by train or bus.
  • By train. Salzburg Hauptbahnhof has direct trains from Munich (about 1.5 hours) and Vienna (about 2.5 hours).
